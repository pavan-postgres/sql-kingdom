# SQL Kingdom – 60-day roadmap

**Format:** 1 hr/day = 30 min story + 30 min hands-on practice.

**World:** Mia (the learner) and Pico (helper robot = the database). Cupboards = tables, shelves = rows, labels = columns.

**End goal:** explain Nested Loop / Hash / Merge joins and query plans from first principles, in plain words.

## Phase 1 – Toy Playroom (Days 1–10): Reading data

- **Day 1 · Cupboards, shelves & labels** – Tables · rows · columns · SELECT
- **Day 2 · Asking for just the right toys** – WHERE
- **Day 3 · Combining wishes** – AND · OR · NOT · IN · BETWEEN
- **Day 4 · Fuzzy memories** – LIKE & patterns
- **Day 5 · Lining things up** – ORDER BY · LIMIT
- **Day 6 · The empty slot** – NULL
- **Day 7 · Nicknames & no repeats** – AS · DISTINCT
- **Day 8 · Adding new toys** – INSERT
- **Day 9 · Fixing & tidying (safely)** – UPDATE · DELETE · undo safety
- **Day 10 · Build your own cupboard** – CREATE TABLE · data types · mini project

## Phase 2 – Counting Party (Days 11–17): Summaries & grouping

- **Day 11 · Counting toys** – COUNT · SUM · AVG · MIN · MAX
- **Day 12 · Sorting into baskets** – GROUP BY
- **Day 13 · Only the big baskets** – HAVING
- **Day 14 · If this, then that** – CASE WHEN
- **Day 15 · Word tricks** – String functions
- **Day 16 · Calendar tricks** – Dates & times
- **Day 17 · Checkpoint** – Recap + mini project

## Phase 3 – Friends Between Cupboards (Days 18–28): Keys & joins

- **Day 18 · Name tags that never repeat** – Primary keys
- **Day 19 · Pointing at a friend** – Foreign keys
- **Day 20 · Matching pairs** – INNER JOIN
- **Day 21 · Keep everyone** – LEFT JOIN
- **Day 22 · The other sides** – RIGHT · FULL JOIN
- **Day 23 · Every possible pair** – CROSS JOIN
- **Day 24 · Mirror, mirror** – Self join
- **Day 25 · Stacking lists** – UNION · INTERSECT · EXCEPT
- **Day 26 · Questions inside questions** – Subqueries
- **Day 27 · Sticky notes** – CTEs (WITH)
- **Day 28 · Checkpoint** – Joins project

## Phase 4 – Super Powers (Days 29–38): Windows, views, transactions

- **Day 29 · The ranking race** – Window functions: ROW_NUMBER · RANK
- **Day 30 · Running totals & neighbours** – SUM OVER · LAG · LEAD
- **Day 31 · Rewind the clock** – Recursive CTEs
- **Day 32 · Saved questions** – Views
- **Day 33 · Rules of the house** – Constraints
- **Day 34 · All or nothing** – Transactions · COMMIT · ROLLBACK
- **Day 35 · ACID in a toy shop** – Atomicity · Consistency · Isolation · Durability
- **Day 36 · Who goes first?** – Isolation levels
- **Day 37 · Boxes inside boxes** – JSON & arrays
- **Day 38 · Checkpoint** – Super powers project

## Phase 5 – Inside the Engine (Days 39–52): How SQL really runs (plans, scans, join algorithms)

- **Day 39 · The kitchen behind the door** – Parser → Planner → Executor
- **Day 40 · Pages & the heap** – How rows sit on disk
- **Day 41 · Walking every shelf** – Sequential scan
- **Day 42 · The book index** – B-tree indexes
- **Day 43 · Jumping with the index** – Index scan · index-only scan
- **Day 44 · Collecting bookmarks** – Bitmap scan
- **Day 45 · The planner’s notebook** – Statistics & cost
- **Day 46 · Reading the plan** – EXPLAIN · EXPLAIN ANALYZE
- **Day 47 · Knock, knock, who’s there?** – Nested Loop join
- **Day 48 · The sorting hat & the sack** – Hash join
- **Day 49 · The zipper** – Merge join
- **Day 50 · Who joins whom first?** – Join order
- **Day 51 · Sorting & grouping inside** – Sort · HashAggregate
- **Day 52 · Checkpoint** – Read and explain a real plan

## Phase 6 – Hero Level (Days 53–60): Production-grade internals & tuning

- **Day 53 · Many readers, no fights** – MVCC
- **Day 54 · The night janitor** – VACUUM
- **Day 55 · The diary that saves you** – WAL & crash recovery
- **Day 56 · Traffic lights** – Locks
- **Day 57 · Making slow fast** – The tuning playbook
- **Day 58 · Slicing giant cupboards** – Partitioning
- **Day 59 · Twin cupboards** – Replication
- **Day 60 · Hero capstone** – Explain any query, inside out

## Per-day recipe

- Content: scenes, words, practice tasks, quiz (scorecard)
- A 3D stage per day, with soothing background music (mute button)
- Teacher notes + LinkedIn caption (private repo)
- Reel on demand, with or without audio (private repo)
- Released one day at a time by the teacher
